Privacy Policy — Catholic Background

Catholic Background ("the app") is available for Windows, KDE Plasma and GNOME. This policy explains what data is processed when you use it.

Who is responsible

{{ opts.PRIVACY_CONTROLLER }}. Contact: {{ opts.PRIVACY_CONTACT }}

What the app does

To show the image of the day, the app periodically requests it from this server. The request only contains today's date. The app does not ask for, collect or send your name, e-mail, location or any other personal information, and it has no account or login. Its settings stay on your computer.

What this server records

For each request the server records the IP address of the computer that made it, the date and time and the technical information sent by the app (such as its version). An IP address is personal data under the GDPR.

Why

1. To detect and fix problems with the service (debugging and security). 2. To produce statistics on how many people use the app. Statistics are aggregated and do not identify anyone. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How long

Full IP addresses are kept for a maximum of {{ opts.ACCESS_LOG_DAYS }} days and then deleted. Statistics are kept without IP addresses (daily counts only) and may be kept indefinitely.

Who we share it with

Nobody. The data is not sold or shared with third parties and it is not used for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, rectification or erasure of your data, restriction of or objection to its processing, by writing to {{ opts.PRIVACY_CONTACT }}. Since only IP addresses are stored, you may need to tell us your IP address and the dates you used the app. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es). If you do not want your IP address to be recorded, do not use the app.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page.
